# Asset credits and licences

All third-party assets are from **[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)** and are released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** — no attribution required, commercial use allowed. Credits are listed anyway.

Raw downloads are fetched by `tools/fetch_assets.py` into `assets-src/` (git-ignored) and packed by `tools/build_assets.mjs` into `assets/` (GLB + KTX2/Basis Universal textures, meshopt geometry, simplified `_lod1` meshes).

## Generated in code

- Station architecture (vaults, pylons with arched passages, cornices, ribs, platform coping, track bed, tunnels, rails, sleepers, contact rail, cable runs), shacks (corrugated sheet and plank construction), stairs, cables, rubble mounds, mushrooms, painted signs, fabric textures, weathering noise and all sound effects are procedural and part of this repository.
