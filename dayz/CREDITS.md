- three.js r180 — MIT — `vendor/three/LICENSE`
- ez-tree procedural tree generator (geometry port in `src/vendor/eztree/`), presets and leaf textures — MIT, © 2024 Daniel Greenheck — `src/vendor/eztree/LICENSE`
- Bark textures shipped with ez-tree: birch & pine from TextureCan (CC0, https://www.texturecan.com), oak from Poly Haven `bark_brown_02` (CC0)
- KTX-Software `toktx` and glTF-Transform are used offline only (not shipped).

Everything else (house, fence, power line, terrain, grass, water, audio) is generated by this project's code.
