    RUSTWATER

    Nine years after the collapse. One crossroads. Whoever holds it, lives.

    Who runs the settlement?

    How to play

    Keep the survivors at the crossroads alive: shelter, water, food, then defences against the dead, then power and the radio mast that calls the evacuation convoy (48 hours on air).

    Two ways to run it

    You command: place every structure, set each survivor's job, send people where you want them. AI Overseer: a planner does all of that and writes its reasoning in the log. Switch at any time (Tab / the AI button); survivors you give direct orders to are locked to you.

    Desktop

    Drag or WASD to pan · right-drag or Q/E to rotate · wheel to zoom · click to select · right-click with a survivor selected to order (move, chop a tree, scavenge a building, build a site, attack) · B build categories, 1–3 speed, Space pause, C colony, H camp, V first person, F follow, Esc menu.

    Touch

    One finger pans, two fingers pinch-zoom and twist to rotate · tap to select · with a survivor selected, tap a target to give an order · Build opens the construction sheet: tap to position, ✓ to confirm, long-press or ↻ to rotate.

    First person

    Take control of a survivor to walk the camp at eye level: shoot, chop, search houses and help build by hand.
